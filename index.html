Wellness & Self-Care

Dress Code for Different Jobs

  • Corporate/Office: Blazer, formal shirt/blouse, tailored pants or pencil skirt, closed shoes, minimal jewelry.
  • Teaching: Modest kurti/saree or formal top with pants, comfortable flats, light makeup.
  • Healthcare: Scrubs/uniform, hair tied up, no dangling jewelry, closed-toe shoes.
  • Retail/Sales: Smart casual, brand colors, name tag, neat grooming, comfortable shoes.
  • IT/Tech: Casual (jeans, t-shirt/hoodie) but neat; sneakers allowed.
  • Creative/Media: Expressive, trendy, statement pieces; smart-casual balance.
  • Field/Outdoor: Durable fabrics, sun protection, closed shoes, hat/cap.
  • Remote/Home: Comfortable yet camera-ready top; avoid overly casual bottoms for calls.

Mood Elevation Tips

  • Start day with 5 min gratitude journaling.
  • Listen to upbeat music or a favorite podcast.
  • Get 10-15 min sunlight and fresh air.
  • Connect with a friend or loved one.
  • Do one small task to build momentum.
  • Limit doom-scrolling; set phone breaks.
  • Reward yourself with a small treat after tasks.
  • Practice deep breathing (4-7-8) when stressed.
  • Wear colors you love; dress up a little.
  • End day with a relaxing hobby (reading, art).

Menstrual Pain & Migraine Relief

Menstrual cramps:

  • Apply heat pad/hot water bottle on lower abdomen.
  • Gentle yoga: child's pose, cat-cow, reclined twist.
  • Drink warm water, ginger/peppermint tea.
  • Light exercise/walk releases endorphins.
  • Magnesium-rich foods (dark chocolate, nuts, spinach).
  • Over-the-counter pain relief as advised by doctor.
  • Avoid excess caffeine/salty foods; stay hydrated.

Migraine:

  • Rest in a dark, quiet room; cold compress on forehead.
  • Hydrate; avoid triggers (bright light, strong smells, skipped meals).
  • Caffeine in small amount may help (if not a trigger).
  • Gentle neck/shoulder stretches; correct posture.
  • Track triggers in a diary; consult neurologist if frequent.
Consult a doctor for persistent/severe pain.

Daily Morning Self-Care

  • Wake at a consistent time; avoid snoozing.
  • Drink a glass of water first thing.
  • 5-10 min stretching or yoga.
  • Wash face; moisturize; apply SPF.
  • Healthy breakfast (protein + fiber).
  • Set 3 priorities for the day.
  • 10 min mindfulness/meditation.
  • Light exposure: open curtains / step outside.
  • Skincare: cleanse, tone, moisturize, SPF.
  • Positive affirmation before starting work.

Yoga Practice (Beginner Friendly)

  • Mountain (Tadasana): posture & grounding.
  • Downward Dog: stretches whole body.
  • Child's Pose: relaxes back & hips.
  • Cat-Cow: spine mobility.
  • Warrior I & II: strength & balance.
  • Tree Pose: focus & stability.
  • Bridge Pose: strengthens glutes/back.
  • Seated Forward Bend: calms mind.
  • Corpse (Savasana): final relaxation.

Practice 15-30 min daily; breathe deeply; don't force. Consult instructor if pregnant/injured.

Bedtime Self-Care Routine

  • Set a consistent sleep time; aim 7-9 hours.
  • Dim lights & reduce screen use 1 hour before bed.
  • Gentle stretching or 5-min meditation.
  • Warm shower/bath to relax muscles.
  • Skincare: cleanse, moisturize, lip balm.
  • Write down tomorrow's 3 priorities to clear mind.
  • Keep bedroom cool, dark, quiet; use fan/white noise.
  • Avoid heavy meals, caffeine, alcohol late at night.
  • Read a few pages or listen to calm music.
  • Gratitude reflection: 3 good things from the day.

Healthy Eating Behaviour

  • Eat mindfully: slow down, chew well, no screens.
  • Half plate veggies/fruits, quarter protein, quarter whole grains.
  • Drink water before meals; stay hydrated all day.
  • Include lean protein, healthy fats (nuts, olive oil), fiber.
  • Limit sugar, ultra-processed foods, excess salt.
  • Don't skip breakfast; keep regular meal times.
  • Portion control: use smaller plates; stop at 80% full.
  • Snack smart: fruits, yogurt, nuts instead of chips.
  • Plan meals ahead; keep healthy options ready.
  • Listen to hunger cues; avoid emotional eating.

Everyday 10-Minute Walk

  • Walk 10 min daily — morning, lunch, or evening.
  • Improves mood, circulation, digestion, sleep quality.
  • Helps weight management & reduces stress.
  • Walk briskly but comfortably; swing arms.
  • Use stairs, park farther, walk while on calls.
  • Track steps with phone; set small goals.
  • Walk with a friend or listen to podcast/music.
  • Wear comfortable shoes; stay hydrated.
  • Even 10 min adds up: ~1,000-1,200 steps.
  • Make it a habit: same time each day.
Informational only; consult professionals for medical/fitness advice.
